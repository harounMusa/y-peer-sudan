Who We Are

A youth-led movement for change, rooted in peer education and community action.

Y-PEER Khartoum is a youth-led peer education network operating under the global Y-PEER (Youth Peer Education Network) initiative, pioneered by UNFPA (United Nations Population Fund).

Our network is dedicated to advancing Sexual and Reproductive Health and Rights (SRHR), promoting gender equality, and building sustainable peace in communities across Sudan. We believe in the power of young people to create lasting change when equipped with accurate information, essential skills, and meaningful opportunities.

We are currently in the process of official registration with the Humanitarian Aid Commission (HAC), formalizing our commitment to structured, accountable youth engagement in Sudan's development.

Our Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To empower young people in Sudan with knowledge, skills, and opportunities to lead healthy lives, advocate for their rights, and drive positive social change in their communities through peer education.

Our Vision

A Sudan where every young person is informed, empowered, and actively participating in building a just, healthy, and peaceful society — free from discrimination and inequality.

Our Values

Youth Leadership

Young people lead every aspect of our work — from design to delivery to evaluation.

Inclusion

We create safe spaces for all young people regardless of gender, background, or identity.

Safeguarding

Protecting the well-being of young people is at the heart of everything we do.

Transparency

We operate with openness and accountability to our communities and partners.
