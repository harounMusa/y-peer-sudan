What We Do

Peer-led programs tackling the most critical issues facing young people in Sudan.

Our Program Areas

Through peer education and community engagement, we tackle the most pressing issues facing young people in Sudan.

Sexual & Reproductive Health

Comprehensive SRHR education, family planning awareness, and access to youth-friendly health services. We train peer educators to deliver accurate information on reproductive health, consent, and healthy relationships.

GBV Prevention

Gender-based violence prevention, survivor support referrals, and community advocacy for women's safety. We work with communities to challenge harmful norms and support survivors.

HIV/AIDS Awareness

Stigma reduction, prevention education, voluntary testing promotion, and support for those living with HIV. Our peer-led approach makes HIV education accessible and non-judgmental.

Mental Health

Mental health awareness, psychosocial support, peer counseling, and combating stigma around mental well-being. We create safe spaces for young people to talk about mental health.

Youth Leadership & Advocacy

Building the next generation of youth leaders through training in advocacy, public speaking, project management, and policy engagement. We amplify youth voices in decision-making spaces.

Peacebuilding & Social Cohesion

Promoting dialogue, conflict resolution, and social cohesion among diverse youth communities. We facilitate inter-community exchanges and peace education workshops.

Our Approach

1

Train the Trainer

We recruit and train youth peer educators using evidence-based curricula, equipping them with facilitation, communication, and counseling skills.

2

Peer-to-Peer Outreach

Trained peer educators reach young people in schools, universities, and community centers through workshops, awareness sessions, and one-on-one conversations.

3

Community Action

Youth-led initiatives translate learning into action — from community clean-ups and health campaigns to advocacy meetings with local leaders.
