Y-PEER Khartoum is a youth-led peer education network advancing sexual and reproductive health, gender equality, and peacebuilding across Sudan. We equip young people with knowledge, skills, and opportunities to drive community transformation.

What We Do

Through peer education and community engagement, we tackle the most pressing issues facing young people in Sudan.

Sexual & Reproductive Health

Comprehensive SRHR education, family planning awareness, and access to youth-friendly health services.

GBV Prevention

Gender-based violence prevention, survivor support referrals, and community advocacy for women's safety.

HIV/AIDS Awareness

Stigma reduction, prevention education, voluntary testing promotion, and support for those living with HIV.

Mental Health

Mental health awareness, psychosocial support, peer counseling, and combating stigma around mental well-being.
