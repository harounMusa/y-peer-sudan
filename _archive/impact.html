Our Impact

Key initiatives that demonstrate the power of youth-led action in Sudan.

UNFPA · 2022

Voices from Sudan

A youth-led documentary and storytelling project amplifying the experiences of young people in Sudan. Produced in partnership with UNFPA, this initiative captured authentic narratives on SRHR, displacement, and resilience, creating a platform for youth voices in national policy dialogues.

Impact: Policy influence & awareness
Community Research

COVID-19 Youth Research

A community-led, youth-powered research project examining the impact of COVID-19 on young people in Sudan. Youth researchers collected and analyzed data on mental health, economic hardship, and access to services, producing evidence that informed local and international response strategies.

Impact: Evidence-based policy
Capacity Building

Peer Educator Training Program

A structured training program that has equipped over 500 young people with facilitation skills, SRHR knowledge, and counseling techniques. Graduates of the program have gone on to lead community workshops, school outreach sessions, and awareness campaigns across Khartoum and beyond.

Impact: 500+ trained peer leaders
Health Campaigns

Community Health Outreach

Mobile health awareness campaigns reaching underserved communities in and around Khartoum. Topics include family planning, menstrual health, HIV prevention, and COVID-19 safety. Our peer educators distribute supplies and provide one-on-one counseling in community settings.

Impact: 10,000+ youth reached
